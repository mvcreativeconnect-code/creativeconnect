About the studio

A small team, obsessed with the shot

CreativeConnect is a photo and video studio based in Malé, working island to island across the Maldives — beach weddings, resort and brand launches, events, and portraits.

We keep the team small on purpose. The person you plan with is the person behind the camera on the day, so nothing gets lost in a handover. We shoot photo and video side by side, which keeps your story consistent across the gallery and the highlight reel.

Every booking starts with a short chat about what you want to remember, followed by a written quote that confirms the crew, location, travel, deliverables and timeline. When the edit is ready, you get a private online gallery to download and share with everyone who was there.

Based in the Maldives but happy to travel, we've photographed everything from intimate beach ceremonies to full resort brand shoots. Whatever the brief, the goal stays the same: images and film you'll actually want to look back on.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Everything couples and clients usually ask before booking. Still unsure? Just send us a message.

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings and peak-season dates, two to three months ahead is ideal — but reach out any time and we'll always try to make your date work.

Do you travel between islands?

Yes. We're based in Malé and travel island-wide. Any transfer or overnight costs are laid out clearly in your quote — no surprises.

When will we get our photos and film?

It depends on the package — typically five to ten days for edited photos, with the highlight film following shortly after. You'll get a few sneak-peek shots much sooner.

Do you shoot photo and video together?

Always. One team covers both, so your gallery and your film feel like a single, consistent story rather than two separate shoots.

How do we book?

Send a request through the booking page with your date and shoot type. We review it and send a written quote confirming crew, location, travel, deliverables and timeline. Nothing is booked until you approve it.

What happens if it rains?

We keep an eye on the forecast and build flexibility into the plan — and honestly, some of our favourite shots happen in soft, overcast light.

Got a date in mind?

Send your brief and we'll come back with a written quote. No payment needed to enquire.
