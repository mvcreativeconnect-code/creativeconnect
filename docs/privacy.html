Privacy & cookies

Your privacy

Plain-language summary of what happens with the details you share. This is a demo academic project, but we've written it the way a real site should.

What we collect

When you send a booking request we collect only what the form asks for:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone / WhatsApp number (optional)
  • Your shoot type
  • Your preferred date
  • The package you chose

Why we collect it

We use these details for one purpose: to reply to your booking request with a shoot plan and quote. We don't sell your data or use it for anything else.

Where it's stored

Booking requests are stored in our own booking system — a private database on the server that runs this site. Only our team can sign in to it, and it is used purely to manage enquiries. If you have given us an email address, we may also email you a copy of your request.

Analytics cookies

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site (for example, which packages get the most interest). These analytics cookies only run after you accept them in the cookie banner. Until then, analytics storage stays denied under Google Consent Mode. You can change your choice any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer.

How to ask for deletion

Want us to delete the details you sent? Email mvcreativeconnect@gmail.com and we'll remove them.

About this project

CreativeConnect is a demonstration website built for a Mianz College digital-marketing assignment. The reviews, prices and photos are placeholders for academic purposes.
